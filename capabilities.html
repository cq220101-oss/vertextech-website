Parts We Make

Typical work from the shop floor. We build to your drawing — these are examples, not a catalog.

Assembly & Test Pallets / Fixtures

Production-line pallets and test fixtures for automotive display (LCD) assembly — machined aluminum bases with locating pins, spring clamps and pneumatic components, built and assembled in series.

Hairpin Motor Tooling

Radial tooling for flat-wire (hairpin) EV motor stators — dozens of precision-ground blades held to tight slot and angle tolerances around a machined hub.

Pneumatic Clamping Fixtures

Fixture plates with integrated cylinders, rotary clamps and air lines, verified on the CMM before delivery so they drop straight into your line.

Precision Machined Plates & Blocks

Aluminum and steel plates, rails and manifold blocks with drilled and tapped hole patterns, measured feature-by-feature against the drawing.
