Every Order Is Measured Before It Leaves the Workshop

You receive measured data with your parts, not just a promise.

  1. 01Drawing reviewWe check tolerances, materials and finishes and raise questions before quoting.
  2. 02Material checkMaterial verified against the drawing and its certificate on arrival.
  3. 03First-article checkFirst parts measured in full before the batch runs.
  4. 04In-process checksCritical features measured during production.
  5. 05Final inspectionCMM and gauge inspection; report supplied with the shipment.

  • 2 CMMs (Hexagon, AEH) — 1000×800×600 mm, 0.001 mm resolution
