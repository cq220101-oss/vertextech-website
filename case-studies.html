Case Studies

A look at how OEM teams use VertexTech to reduce sourcing risk and keep production moving.

AUTOMOTIVE MANUFACTURING · MEXICO

Consolidating a Tier 1 Supplier's Machined Component Sourcing

Situation

A Tier 1 automotive supplier in Querétaro was managing several small machine shops directly for CNC-machined tooling components, each with its own lead time, documentation format, and delivery terms.

Solution

VertexTech became the single purchasing contact, sourcing the components to the released drawing revision, supplying EN 10204 3.1 material certificates and dimensional reports with every delivery under DDP terms.

Result

The customer's purchasing team went from coordinating multiple vendors to issuing one PO per program, with consistent documentation on every shipment.

1
Purchasing contact, down from 4 vendors
18 days
Standard lead time on released parts
INDUSTRIAL MACHINERY

Emergency Resourcing After a Supplier Shutdown

Situation

An industrial machinery manufacturer's turned-components supplier closed with three weeks' notice, putting a production line at risk of stopping.

Solution

VertexTech identified and qualified an alternative supplier within its network, matched to the same material and tolerance requirements, and expedited the first production run.

Result

Parts shipped before the customer's existing stock ran out, with no gap in the production schedule.

10 days
From request to first shipment
0
Production line stoppages
MATERIAL HANDLING

Consolidating MRO and Production Components Under One Vendor

Situation

A material handling equipment manufacturer was sourcing MRO consumables and safety equipment separately from its mechanical components, adding vendor overhead for the purchasing team.

Solution

VertexTech brought MRO supplies, safety equipment, and mechanical components under a single account, with consolidated invoicing and one delivery schedule.

Result

The purchasing team reduced the number of active vendor accounts and simplified monthly reconciliation.

Fewer active vendor accounts
1
Consolidated delivery schedule
