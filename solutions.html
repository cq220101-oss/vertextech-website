How Sourcing Works

From drawing to delivery, in four steps

1. Send Your Drawing

Share your print, quantity, and target delivery date — PDF or STEP file, any revision level.

2. We Source It

We match your requirement to a qualified supplier in our network and issue a formal quotation.

3. Production & Quality Check

Parts are produced to the released revision, with dimensional and material checks before shipment.

4. Delivered to Your Dock

Consolidated shipment under the agreed Incoterms, with certification and inspection reports included.
