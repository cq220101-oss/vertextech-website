Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No. We machine single parts, prototypes and small batches. One piece is a normal order.

What lead time should I expect for a low-volume order?

Around 20 days is typical for standard machined parts. The exact lead time depends on material, finish and complexity and is confirmed in writing with your quotation.

What tolerances can you hold?

Our CNC machining centers hold tolerances down to ±0.005 mm, and precision surface grinding reaches ±0.002 mm. Tell us which features are critical and we review them before quoting.

Do single parts get an inspection report?

Yes. Every order is dimensionally checked before shipping, and the inspection report is supplied with the parts.

Which files do you need for a quote?

A 3D model (STEP) and a 2D drawing (PDF or DWG) with tolerances, material and finish. You can upload them here; we reply within one business day and can sign an NDA on request.

Can you ship to the U.S. and Mexico?

Yes. We export pack every order and offer DDP delivery to the U.S. and Mexico, with online tracking by PO number.

Need a few parts machined?

Send your drawing with the quantity you need, even if it's one. We'll come back with a formal quotation.
